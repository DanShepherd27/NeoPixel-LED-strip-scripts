The glowing previews brighten dark colors on screen; the exact RGB values shown are sent to the LEDs.

Six band spectrum animates color-coded frequency zones: sub-bass, bass, low mids, mids, high mids, and treble. They respond independently, so kicks and cymbals can move different parts of the strip; it reacts to frequency ranges rather than identifying individual instruments. Retro VU exaggerates volume and drum peaks, with green, yellow, and red zones. Inward diamonds send shapes from both ends toward the center.

Each pattern shows the color controls it uses. All six band colors are customizable in Six band spectrum.
