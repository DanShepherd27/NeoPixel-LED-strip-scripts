The glowing previews brighten dark colors on screen; the exact RGB values shown are sent to the LEDs.

Live Band Spectrum animates six color-coded frequency zones: sub-bass, bass, low mids, mids, high mids, and treble. They respond independently, so kicks and cymbals can move different parts of the strip; it reacts to frequency ranges rather than identifying individual instruments. Retro VU exaggerates volume and drum peaks, with green, yellow, and red zones. Inward diamonds send shapes from both ends toward the center.

Primary and accent colors apply to Center pulse, Mirror bars, Comet, and Inward diamonds. Rainbow, Retro VU, Live Band Spectrum, and Red Shades keep their own palettes.
